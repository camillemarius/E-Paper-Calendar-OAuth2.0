Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

E-Paper Calendar

E-Paper Calendar is a personal electronic paper calendar display. The application uses the Google Calendar API to retrieve calendar events and display them on a local e-paper display.

Google Calendar Data

E-Paper Calendar accesses Google Calendar data only to retrieve and display calendar events on the user's personal e-paper display. The application does not sell, share, or distribute Google Calendar data to third parties.

Calendar data retrieved from Google is processed locally on the E-Paper Calendar device and is not transmitted to any external service other than the Google APIs required to provide the calendar functionality.

Authentication

E-Paper Calendar uses Google's OAuth 2.0 authentication to obtain permission to access the user's Google Calendar. Authentication tokens are stored locally on the E-Paper Calendar device and are used only to authenticate requests to Google APIs.

Data Storage

E-Paper Calendar does not operate a remote database or cloud service for storing personal calendar information. Calendar information retrieved from Google Calendar is processed locally on the device.

Third-Party Services

E-Paper Calendar uses the Google Calendar API. Google's use of information obtained through Google APIs is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
